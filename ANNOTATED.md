## 03 · THE SECOND COLLECTION

**The conceit:** their debut collection is four fragrances named by negation. We wrote five more — each naming a problem. The compliment is structural: *your naming system is strong enough to name your own weak points.* It criticises, so it can't read as fan mail; it's in their language, so it can't read as an outsider's lecture.

**Skim layer per item:** number → headline → one line → the orange fix line. Everything else is behind `+ Read the case`.

> **"The World of Antonyms has four. Here are five more."** — `THEIRS` (collection name) + `OURS`
> **"They're not for sale. They're already yours."** — `OURS`. Positions the whole page as a gift, not an invoice. Sets up the footer.

## Deliberate absences

- **No prices.** Numbers invite comparison shopping; risk reversal invites a reply. Prices go in the reply.
- **No photography.** We don't have their assets, and generating lookalikes would be forgery. Absence of imagery is the strongest anti-fan-account signal.
- **No city named.** Their HQ is unresolved (Inc42 says Delhi; Kunal's profile says Delhi; NEWME is Bengaluru). One wrong fact would collapse the receipt.
- **No post count, no start date.** The logged-out grid is capped at 12 by Instagram. Post `DX_7dDZxVBj` (6 May 2026, "Stay MIS–READ") proves older content exists.
- **No green anywhere.** Green beside the accent red reads as a clearance-sale palette — an explicit CUNIN anti-pattern. Enforced by `lint.mjs`.
- **No logo wall, no awards, no case studies.** Six lines about us, after the value.
